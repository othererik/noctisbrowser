Broken Promises: Words vs. Actions

Compare public campaign pledges directly against executive orders, budget clawbacks, and verified policy outcomes.

Energy & Utility Costs 2025 – 2026
The Campaign Promise

“I will cut your energy and electricity bills in half within 12 months of taking office.”

Detroit and Atlanta economic speeches.
What Was Actually Enacted

Canceled energy efficiency home rebates and placed heavy tariffs on imported power transformers and solar components, causing regional residential electric utility rates to increase by 8% to 15%.

Veterans & Military 2025 – 2026
The Campaign Promise

“Our great veterans will get the best healthcare in the world, with zero wait times and full funding.”

Veterans of Foreign Wars convention address.
What Was Actually Enacted

Ordered sweeping federal hiring freezes that left over 18,000 Veterans Health Administration (VHA) clinical and mental health posts vacant, leading to canceled specialty care appointments nationwide.

Bridging the Divide

Common Ground: How to Talk to Family & Neighbors

Real change happens through respectful, face-to-face conversations centered on shared American values—rule of law, economic security, honest government, and community well-being.

1.

Lead with Shared Values

Start by acknowledging shared concerns: protecting our families, lower costs, safe communities, and supporting veterans.

2.

Stick to Kitchen-Table Facts

Avoid partisan buzzwords. Discuss real grocery bills, local VA hospital cuts, and small business tariff costs.

3.

Ask Sincere Questions

Invite reflection rather than lecturing: "Do you think any president should have unchecked power without congressional oversight?"
